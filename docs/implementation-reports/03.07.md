## 1. What was implemented

- **Publisher and relay (`internal/outbox`).**
  - The `Publisher` interface: `Publish(ctx, []Message)`, where `Message{Topic, Key, Value, Headers}`.
  - Its contract: per-key order, `*PartialError{Published}` for a delivered prefix, and context honored.
  - Implementations: `LogPublisher`, `DiscardPublisher` and `MemoryPublisher`.
  - `RelayLoop` is leader-gated. In one transaction per batch it asserts the epoch, locks up to 500 unpublished rows in id order (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) and publishes them, bounded by a 10 s `PublishTimeout`. It then marks published only the delivered prefix.
  - Failures retry through `scheduler.RunTicking`'s capped backoff. Delivery is at least once and ordered per run.
  - Metrics: `flowforge_outbox_backlog`, `_oldest_age_seconds`, `_publish_lag_seconds` and `_publish_failures_total`.
  - The publisher is chosen by `FF_EVENT_PUBLISHER=log|none|kafka`; `kafka` is a startup error until 05.01.
- **Events channel.** `events.Recorder.Record` sends `pg_notify('ff_events','')` in the same transaction for every run event (`events.NotifyChannel`).
- **SSE (`internal/api/sse`, wired at `GET /api/v1/events/stream`, viewer).**
  - **Filters and resume:** `run_id`, `types` and `since` filter the stream, and `Last-Event-ID` resumes it.
  - **Format:** `id: <workflow_events.id>\nevent: <type>\ndata: <envelope>\n\n`. The envelope comes from the outbox, or is rebuilt once the outbox row is gone.
  - **Wake-ups:** one shared `LISTEN ff_events` connection (a `workerapi.Waker`) is fanned out by `sse.Hub`, plus a 500 ms poll.
  - **Keep-alive and limits:** a keep-alive comment every 15 s; `FF_SSE_MAX_CONNECTIONS` (200), with 503 and Retry-After beyond it; a 10 s per-write deadline drops slow clients.
  - **Timeouts and shutdown:** the stream is exempt from the request timeout and clears the server's read deadline. `Hub.Close` runs on server shutdown.
  - **Commit horizon:** the stream never moves past an id that may still commit.
- **Retention (`internal/retention`).**
  - `Loop` is leader-gated and runs hourly. It deletes published outbox rows older than 3 days, `workflow_events` older than 30 days and `task_logs` older than 14 days, and clears `workflow_runs.idempotency_key` for runs older than 24 h.
  - It works in batches of at most 5,000 (`ORDER BY id LIMIT`), with pauses between batches.
  - `FF_RETENTION_*` overrides the ages. The metric is `flowforge_retention_deleted_rows_total{kind}`.
- **Run events cursor.** `GET /runs/{id}/events` already paged by `workflow_events.id` (02.04). It is the same id SSE uses, so no change was needed (documented).
- **Wiring.** The relay and retention loops are registered in `flowforge-scheduler`. The events waker, `sse.Handler` and `Hub.Close` on shutdown are wired in `flowforge-api`. OpenAPI and the ACL are updated.

## 5. Architectural decisions made during implementation

- **Reporting a partial delivery.** The publisher reports a delivered prefix through `*PartialError`. Any other error means nothing can be assumed delivered. This keeps "mark the published prefix" exact.
- **One relay transaction.** Epoch assert, lock, publish and mark happen in one transaction, so a deposed leader can neither mark rows nor reorder them.
- **The SSE commit horizon.** Ids are assigned at insert, so a stream doesn't move past a missing id until it commits, or until it has been missing for 1 s, or until the row after it is older than 30 s. Without this, an event committed after a higher id would be lost for good. The `ecc:go-reviewer` agent found the gap. The same horizon also lets filtered streams advance over events they drop.
- **Reuse instead of a new listener.** The task's `api/sse/listener.go` became a reuse of `workerapi.Waker`, which is already a single-connection LISTEN fan-out (`00C` §2.1).
- **The REST layer owns errors.** It parses the request and maps errors (400, 503 with Retry-After); `sse` only streams, which avoids an import cycle.
- **Retention metric label.** It uses the already allowed `kind` label rather than extending `obs.AllowedLabels`.

## 7. Known limitations / follow-ups

- **NOTIFY serializes commits.** Postgres takes a global lock at commit for transactions that sent NOTIFY. Measured at 24 concurrent writers it adds about 1 ms p50 and keeps p99 under 10 ms. If it ever limits throughput, the 500 ms poll alone still delivers.
- **A hung publisher.** One that ignores `ctx` would hold the relay's transaction and the epoch share lock. The contract requires honoring `ctx`, and 05.01 must.
- **Rebuilt envelopes lack `producer`.** A global `since` without `run_id` scans `workflow_events` from the start on its first read. Resuming after retention removed events gives no gap marker. A new live-only stream can miss an event committed at the same moment it starts.
- **Old history loses an invariant.** Events older than 30 days are deleted, so `AssertInvariants`' "status matches latest event" holds only for live data.
- **Temporary stubs:** none.
- **Defects found in earlier tasks** (each a separate suggested commit):
  - **03.06 `template.Refs`** returned references in random order, because it iterated a Go map. `TestRefs` failed 2 of 5 runs. **Fix:** `walk` visits object keys in sorted order; `TestRefs` then passed 50 of 50.
  - **03.04 recovery harness `mustPoll`** gave a claim one 300 ms real-time window. Under the heavier 03.07 full suite, three different run-control tests failed on their first poll with "polled 0 tasks", with setup alone taking about 3.7 s. They never failed in isolation (5 runs).
    - **Ruled out:** a probe showed NOTIFY adds about 1 ms of commit latency at 24 concurrent writers, nowhere near 300 ms.
    - **Fix:** `mustPoll` now polls until it has the wanted tasks, up to 10 s. Polls that must find nothing still use `poll`'s short window. The final full run passed 32 of 32.
- **Agent review findings fixed:**
  - the commit horizon, and filtered streams advancing their cursor;
  - `Hub.Close` on shutdown;
  - read-failure logging once per transition;
  - a startup warning for `log` and `none`;
  - `errors.Join` of the publish and commit errors;
  - no backlog warning at shutdown;
  - retention continuing past a failing target and stopping quietly on shutdown;
  - poison frames skipped.
