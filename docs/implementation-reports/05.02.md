## 1. What was implemented

- **`internal/stream/domain`** (pure, no I/O):
  - **Ticks:** `Tick` (decimal `Price` and `Size`) and `Validate(tick, now) Reason`, with the six validation reasons plus `too_late`.
  - **Windows:** `WindowSize` (`1m`, `5m`), `Window`, and `WindowFor`, aligned to UTC epoch boundaries and half-open.
  - **Aggregation:** `Aggregate(ticks, window) Stats`, which is order-independent (open and close by `(event_time, seq, event_id)`), computes OHLCV, VWAP and count, and a SHA-256 `input_hash` of the sorted event ids.
  - **Indicators:** `MovingAverage`, `Volatility` (sample std-dev of log returns) and `DetectAnomaly` (z-score; n = 20, k = 3, at least 10 samples).
  - **Lateness:** `Watermark` and `Classify` → `OnTime` / `LateRecompute` / `TooLate`.
- **Purity is lint-enforced:** the new depguard rule `stream-domain-pure` keeps net, os, randomness and every FlowForge package out of the package's non-test code. `make lint-selftest` proves it fires.
- **TimescaleDB migrations** (`migrations/marketdata/0001`–`0004`):
  - `market_ticks` (hypertable on `event_time`, unique `(event_id, event_time)`, `numeric` prices, `late`);
  - `market_aggregates` (hypertable, PK `(symbol, window_size, window_start)`, `status`, `version`, `input_hash`);
  - `market_ingest_state` and `market_replay_jobs`;
  - compression after 2 days and retention of 14 days on ticks.
- **`docs/architecture/market-data-math.md`:** every formula, the three worked examples that are the test vectors, the lateness table and the schema rationale.

## 5. Architectural decisions made during implementation

- **Exact prices:** `shopspring/decimal` in Go and `numeric` in the database. Only volatility and z-scores, which are statistics of log returns, are floats. Derived prices (VWAP, moving average) have 8 places.
- **Lateness is judged in event time only:** `Classify` takes no `now`, so a replay classifies as the live run did.
- **Zero size is valid:** it lets the VWAP-of-zero-volume rule apply; the published `market_tick.v1` schema still requires size > 0 at the Kafka edge.
- **`market_ingest_state` is keyed by consumer group, topic and partition,** so a replay group can't overwrite the live group's row.

## 7. Known limitations / follow-ups

- **Indicator precision:** volatility and z-scores are `float64` (`math.Log`), so they are not bit-exact across platforms; stored prices are exact.
- **`Aggregate` doesn't dedupe:** it relies on the table's `(event_id, event_time)` key.
- **Defects found in earlier tasks:** 05.01's `kafkatest.Topic` returned before the broker listed the new topic. Under load, `TestConsumer_RedeliversUncommitted`'s first produce failed with `UNKNOWN_TOPIC_OR_PARTITION`. Minimal fix: the helper waits until every partition has a leader. It's a separate suggested commit (§9); there is no deterministic reproduction, and the evidence is the failing run plus 3/3 after.
